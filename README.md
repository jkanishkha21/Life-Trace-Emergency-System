#  Life Trace – Smart Emergency Location Alert System

##  Overview

Life Trace is a smart emergency alert system designed to provide automatic assistance during critical situations by sending location-based alerts to emergency contacts.

The system aims to reduce response time during emergencies when a person may not be able to manually call for help.

##  Features

-  Low battery emergency alert trigger
-  GPS-based location sharing
-  SMS emergency notification
-  Voice confirmation support
-  Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) tracking concept
-  Future smartwatch integration

## Technologies & Concepts Used

- GPS Location Services
- SMS Communication
- Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE)
- Emergency Alert Systems
- Prototype Design

##  Problem Statement

In emergency situations, people may not always be able to unlock their phones and contact others.

Life Trace provides an automated safety mechanism that helps send alerts with location information to trusted contacts.
